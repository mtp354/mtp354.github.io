# Fellowships, Summer Schools, Grants and Awards for CUNY Students

After getting frustrated with finding out about possible grants or opportunities I could apply for only after the deadline has passed, I decided to compile a big list of everything I (or anyone else!) might want to apply for, all in one place. CUNY is great and has a lot of opportunities, but they are often scattered across different websites and internal portals and buried deep email chains somehow always sent to your other CUNY email address. Many of these opportunities should be useful to non-CUNY and non-Physics grad students too! If you are a CUNY student and you want to help, I'd eventually like to turn this into a CUNY survival guide google doc that is perpetually updated, maintained and passed on to future grad students, if that interests you, please let me know! 

A note on eligibility traps, because they decide more applications than the essay does: several of the big national fellowships below require U.S. citizenship or permanent residence, and several require you to be at a specific stage (first year, post-first-exam, Level III, or already a Ph.D. candidate). A few, notably the Unitary Fund microgrant and the LANL summer school, do not. Also check out the Paul and Daisy Soros Fellowship if you are an immigrant/refugee/asylum seeker/child of immigrants or other "Recent American"! I'd like to add details on other resources that are helpful such as the [College Student Pantry](https://sites.google.com/view/collegestudentpantry/?pli=1&authuser=0) in the future, so let me know if you have anything else I should add.

## Microgrants and project grants — including the Unitary Fund microgrant

This is the category most students overlook because it is not a "fellowship" in the CV sense. It is also the fastest to apply for.

| Programme | Apply | Deadline | Best suited for | Purpose | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unitary Foundation Microgrant | [Unitary Foundation](https://unitary.foundation/) | Rolling — no deadline | Anyone, any stage, any country. No organisational affiliation and no credentials required. | A short, no-strings project that leverages quantum technologies to benefit people: open-source quantum software, education materials and workshops, a new quantum sensor prototype, and similar. Projects usually run 3–6 months. Application is a short form plus a two-minute video. | $4,000 cash grant |
