# Fellowships, Summer Schools, Grants and Awards for CUNY Students

_Draft — held from publication for review. Not rendered on the site while in `_drafts/` with `published: false`._

A working list for CUNY doctoral students — especially Graduate Center physics / quantum students, and CUNY adjuncts — pulled together from my Internship Applications spreadsheet (2027 tab), my site's Quantum Radar digest, the Graduate Center's funding pages, the PSC, and the foundation / grant sweeps I run. Deadlines and amounts were checked on 27 October 2026. They change every cycle, so verify on the linked page before you apply. Where a deadline is marked TBD, the programme had not published its next cycle when I checked.

A note on eligibility traps, because they decide more applications than the essay does: several of the big national fellowships below require U.S. citizenship or permanent residence, and several require you to be at a specific stage (first year, post-first-exam, Level III, or already a Ph.D. candidate). A few — notably the Unitary Fund microgrant and the LANL summer school — do not. Read the stage and citizenship lines first.

## CUNY-internal: start here

## Microgrants and project grants — including the Unitary Fund microgrant

This is the category most students overlook because it is not a "fellowship" in the CV sense. It is also the fastest to apply for.

| Programme | Apply | Deadline | Best suited for | Purpose | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unitary Foundation Microgrant | [Unitary Foundation](https://unitary.foundation/) | Rolling — no deadline | Anyone, any stage, any country. No organisational affiliation and no credentials required. | A short, no-strings project that leverages quantum technologies to benefit people: open-source quantum software, education materials and workshops, a new quantum sensor prototype, and similar. Projects usually run 3–6 months. Application is a short form plus a two-minute video. | $4,000 cash grant |

## How to use this list

1. **Apply to the CUNY-internal pots first.** DSRG (31 Jan), the Provost's Pre-Dissertation award (5 Feb) and Conference Presentation Support ($300, every semester) are the highest-probability money on this page, and DSRG in particular is good practice for the bigger proposals.
2. **Check stage and citizenship before you draft anything.** Hertz, GRFP and CSGF are all early-stage and, except for GRFP/CSGF/Hertz citizenship rules, easy to waste a week on if you are past the eligible year.
3. **If you have a concrete quantum project but no lab funding for it, try Unitary first.** It is $4,000, rolling, and does not require an affiliation — the application cost is a form and a two-minute video, which makes it the best effort-to-reward ratio on this page.
4. **For summer 2027, put LANL on a January watch.** It was the only summer school in my list with a paid fellowship in the $10,000–$20,000 range and no citizenship requirement.
5. **Keep a personal deadline sheet.** The GC and PSC forms are semester- or cycle-specific: the link that worked last year is often replaced, and the chair-approval or EO-signature step is the part that actually takes time.
